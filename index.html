Case Study 01

Scaling Through Prime Day Without Losing Control of TACoS

Client Context

An established apparel seller on Amazon US, with an existing PPC account and a hard TACoS ceiling to respect.

The Problem

The account needed a manager who could grow ad spend aggressively enough to capture Prime Day demand — without letting advertising costs eat into margin.

My Approach

Took over management the third week of April 2024. Built a bid and budget cadence tied to a strict 6% TACoS target, then let spend scale ahead of Prime Day while watching ACoS daily.

What I Delivered
  • Bid and budget management held to a 6% TACoS ceiling
  • Coordinated ad scaling ahead of the July Prime Day event
  • Continuous keyword and placement optimization through Q3
The Result
🚀Prime Day daily sales quadrupled vs. the account's average
↑Total sales grew 28% from April to August
→TACoS never crossed the 6% target
